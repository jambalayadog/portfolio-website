## Technical Design

- Built boss encounters, including mega-boss Dreadwood; scaling to support 1-4 players.

- Scripted character abilities and diverse AI behaviors, including an 'unalert' AI system that would be proof of concept for open world PvZ.

- Implemented AI for numerous characters, including bosses and their special abilities

- Built 'DPS box' tool that would eventually be used in automated testing framework to authentically measure damage calculations used for game balance.

- Collaborated with UX engineer to build a data-driven progression system as well as in-game store features which served players across live service lifecycles.

## Monthly Activities

I led the design of live service activities, overseeing one other designer:

- Created a recipe to generate monthly activities like 'Valenbrainz' and 'Luck of the Zombie.'

- Prototyped multiplayer game modes to extend our single-player maps: scripted game modes, organized the playtests, collected & evaluated feedback, and iterated.
