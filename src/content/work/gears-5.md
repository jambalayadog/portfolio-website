Built PvP and PvE maps taking them from concept to release, following [long established Gears of War level design process](https://youtu.be/73Pqsk74Jc0?si=sqw9bTWPN80s_gTa&t=957).

- Greyblocked maps first for macro scale and layout for concept and prototyping, and then for micro details to refine cover systems and gameplay before hand off to Art team

- Configured maps for our PvE and PvP multiplayer modes placing spawners, cinematics, cameras, and lots of work towards navmesh and havok collision

- Maintained gameplay integrity during art production phases, while providing qualitative feedback to art direction to ensure gameplay quality

- Extensively playtested and iterated upon maps pushing for high quality, and I may have even implemented an Easter Egg...

Check out [Village](https://youtu.be/8C6Qsu9ZjbY?t=15), a DLC map from Operation 8 that allows close quarters combat to shine.
