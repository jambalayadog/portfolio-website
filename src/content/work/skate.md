I joined pre-alpha, quickly becoming the POC for challenge implementation.

- Developed over 30 varied challenges, including Pro Challenges, spot challenges, competitions, deathraces, and more.

- Created and exported Maya volumes & locators, and created Lua scripts and expression to evaluate the skaters tricks against geometry and challenge criteria.

- Closely collaborated with environment artists to find spots and tune them, particularly all of Old Town

- Designed text & audio for challenge sign-ups and objectives

- Worked with the art director to validate the visual language of our markers, as well as create custom trick locators using spline tech.
